    Zoning rules for this lot

    District values are for a single-family detached home, from Table 21.06-1 in AMC 21.06.020. Chugiak-Eagle River, Girdwood and Downtown have their own tables and are not included. ADU rules come from AMC 21.05.070D.1: same setbacks as the district, no setback on a side or rear line next to an alley, house plus all accessory buildings within the lot coverage limit, and floor area up to 900 sq ft or 40% of the main house, whichever is greater, never over 1,200 sq ft. In R-1 and R-1A, lots under 10,000 sq ft may go to 50% coverage when the whole house is under 16 ft tall. The gap from buildings is not a zoning rule; set it if your reviewer asks for one.

      What a full version would add

      Flood zone, seismic zone, slope and water and sewer service are looked up from the address, the way the lot lines and building outlines already are. An aerial photo sits under the drawing. Footprints come from the pre-approved plan catalog. Existing surveys on file with the Municipality load without an upload. The result is a one-page slip to bring to the zoning review meeting and hand to your surveyor.
