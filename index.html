    ADU design

    Which footprints fit this lot

    Shadows

    For thinking and discussion only. No shadow rule is checked. The drawing treats the ground as flat and every building as a flat-topped block at the height in the Municipality's building map, which looks to be the highest point of the roof, so building shadows are a little overstated. Trees and fences are not shown, and they often matter more. The plan sheets print no height, so the ADU height starts as a guess: change it to your own.

    Zoning rules for this lot

    District values are for a single-family detached home, from Table 21.06-1 in AMC 21.06.020. Chugiak-Eagle River, Girdwood and Downtown have their own tables and are not included. On a lot with more than one street side, every street line gets the full front setback and there is no rear setback (AMC 21.06.030C.4); the zoning director decides the setbacks on such a lot. ADU rules come from AMC 21.05.070D.1: same setbacks as the district, no setback on a side or rear line next to an alley, house plus all accessory buildings within the lot coverage limit, and floor area, not counting a related garage, up to 900 sq ft or 40% of the main house, whichever is greater, never over 1,200 sq ft. The same section limits an ADU to 25 ft in height, or 30 ft over a garage; this page does not check height. In R-1 and R-1A, lots under 10,000 sq ft may go to 50% coverage when the whole house is under 16 ft tall. The gap from buildings is not a zoning rule; set it if your reviewer asks for one.
